About

Software shaped around real dental lab work

Probotz brings clinic intake, production coordination, compliance documentation, logistics, pricing, and invoicing into a single operating platform.

Built for operational clarity

Dental labs coordinate detailed work across clinics, practitioners, patients, files, materials, production teams, outsourced partners, shipments, and invoices. Probotz is designed for that environment, with traceability and client experience treated as part of the same workflow.

For labs, clinics, and partners

The platform supports multi-tenant organizations, role-based access, branded portals, language settings, manufacturer profiles, and structured communication so each participant sees the right context at the right time.
