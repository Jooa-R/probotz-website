About Probotz

Software starts with understanding the work.

Probotz Consultancy Oy is a focused software company based in Finland. We design and build products and digital services for problems that need more than another generic tool.

Practical by design

A useful system needs to reflect the details of the work: who needs which information, where responsibility changes hands and what happens when the process takes a different route.

We start there. We simplify the workflow, design the experience, build the software and connect external services where needed. Deployment and operation are part of making the solution useful.

One company, more than one kind of work

DentalCharz is our flagship product: a connected operating platform for dental laboratories. It brings intake, production, collaboration, logistics and invoicing into a shared case workflow.

We also take on selected customer projects. Our work for Jousiammuntaseura Falcon applies the same attention to structure and usability to a smaller challenge: a new website for an established archery club, with its history preserved.

Built to fit

The answer might be operational software, a web application, a portal or a clearer website. The aim is to build what the problem calls for and make it work well in practice.
