Association website · Inkoo, Finland

Jousiammuntaseura Falcon

A modern replacement for an ageing Weebly site, designed and developed by Probotz for a Finnish archery association.

The need

A clearer welcome.
A place for history.

Falcon needed a public website that made sense to someone discovering the club for the first time, while keeping years of existing content and historical material accessible.

The work centred on familiar questions: how to start archery, where to train, where to find competition information and how to contact the club.

What Probotz built

A practical new home for the club.

Navigation around visitor needs

A Finnish-first structure with clear routes to starting archery, training, competitions, club information and contacts.

History carried forward

Existing historical material and results documents migrated into a dedicated archive, keeping past achievements accessible.

A responsive club identity

A new interface with a visual identity suited to Falcon, layouts for mobile and desktop, and attention to accessibility.

Deployment and domain setup

Modern deployment and the domain/DNS work needed to bring the new site online.

Project scope

The right size for the problem.

This is a focused association website project: information design, content preservation and a clearer public experience. It shows another side of Probotz's work alongside the more extensive operational software in DentalCharz.

The new public website is being prepared for launch.
