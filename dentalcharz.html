Built around the case

The details stay with the work.

DentalCharz follows a case across people, systems and physical handoffs, so each stage has the context it needs.

01 / Intake

Turn incoming work into a configured case

Receive clinic and practitioner requests with physical or digital impressions, scans and attachments. Configure products and tooth-level prescriptions, with QR codes linking the physical case.

Medit Link integration and synchronisation support scanner-originated intake, automatically or manually, with product mapping to help prepare the draft.

02 / Production

Coordinate the next step

Move cases through statuses and production stages with assignments, priorities, holds and alerts. Keep case conversations and production files attached to the work.

Coordinate lab, clinic and manufacturer handoffs, including outsourced work, turnaround information and delivery expectations.

03 / Logistics

Keep physical work traceable

Organise clinic-to-lab and lab-to-clinic shipments, combine work into shipment batches and prepare labels through the Posti integration.

Physical case codes and component or increment tracking connect what is being made and shipped to the digital case.

04 / Commercial workflow

Invoice the work fulfilled

Build a configurable catalogue with product options, tooth-level prescriptions and clinic-specific price lists. Live estimates give context while an order is configured.

Fulfilment-based invoicing, invoices and credit notes carry the work into billing, with VAT, payment terms and Maventa electronic invoicing.

05 / Clinic portal

Give clinics a clear view

Branded portals bring practitioner and clinic access, case communication, files, status and delivery visibility into one place.

Custom domains, colours, logos and a multilingual interface let the portal fit the laboratory's customer experience.

06 / History & documentation

Keep the record connected

Case history and production records support auditability. Manufacturer information and custom-made device documentation stay connected to the case in supported workflows.
