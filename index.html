Capabilities

A connected operating system for modern dental labs

Probotz groups the moving parts of lab operations into calm, readable workflows for teams, clinics, and manufacturing partners.

Case management

Manage dental lab cases, orders, patient references with aliases and duplicate merges, clinic relationships, impressions, product planning, statuses, turnaround rules, and exports.

Communication and collaboration

Keep case chat, production chat, read states, attachments, internal lab notes, and manufacturer-facing notes connected to the work.

Production and fulfilment

Track production stages, assignment states, materials, batches, line items, priorities, holds, alerts, late files, and outsourced manufacturer metadata.

Compliance and documentation

Generate certificates and proformas for custom-made medical device workflows with managed manufacturer profiles and multilingual declaration text.

Shipping and logistics

Coordinate shipments for clinics, outsource labs, suppliers, and components, including shipment batches, label PDFs, and Posti integration support.

Pricing and invoicing

Use price lists, product pricing, clinic-specific assignments, VAT, payment terms, invoice numbering, PDFs, XML export, and tenant billing settings.

Portal branding and access

Configure tenant logos, colors, email sender identity, custom domains, favicon, roles, entitlements, language settings, and managed translations.

Digital intake integrations

Support scanner-originated cases with Medit Link imports, manufacturer invoice imports, code mapping, and structured file handling.

Account governance

Keep subscriptions, plan entitlements, feature gating, release notes, legal acceptance, privacy and terms tracking, and user account flows organized.

How it works

A clear path through every case

1

Receive cases and impressions

Capture digital or physical intake with clinic, practitioner, patient reference, files, products, and due dates in one place.

2

Coordinate production and communication

Assign work, track statuses, manage alerts, and keep lab, clinic, and manufacturer conversations tied to the case.

3

Manage compliance and shipping

Prepare documentation, fulfilment details, batches, labels, and logistics records before the case leaves the lab.

4

Complete invoicing with traceability

Turn products and line items into invoices with pricing rules, VAT, payment terms, PDFs, XML export, and a full case history.
