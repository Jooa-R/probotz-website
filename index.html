Capabilities

A connected operating system for modern dental labs

Probotz groups the moving parts of lab operations into calm, readable workflows for teams, clinics, and manufacturing partners.

Case management

Manage dental lab cases, orders, patient references with aliases and duplicate merges, clinic relationships, impressions, scans, product planning, statuses, turnaround rules, and exports.

Communication and collaboration

Keep case chat, production chat, read states, attachments, internal lab notes, and manufacturer-facing notes connected to the work.

Production and fulfilment

Track shippable components, batches, production stages, assignments, materials, line items, priorities, holds, alerts, and late files. Keep outsourced parts and components traceable through the entire workflow.

Compliance and documentation

Prepare certificates and compliance documentation for custom-made medical device workflows with managed manufacturer profiles and multilingual declaration text.

Shipping and logistics

Coordinate shipments for clinics, outsource labs, suppliers, and components, including shipment batches, label PDFs, and Posti integration support.

Pricing and real-time estimates

Show real-time price estimates as orders are configured. Calculate pricing from products, options, and case configuration before invoicing, with support for price lists, clinic-specific assignments, VAT, and payment terms.

Portal branding and access

Configure tenant logos, colors, email sender identity, custom domains, favicon, roles, entitlements, language settings, and managed translations.

Scan and file handling

Store, preview, and manage scan files, 3D models, impressions, and case attachments. Use QR code labels to connect physical work to digital cases, and support structured file handling for scanner-originated data.

Multi-user management and access

Manage team members, clinics, and manufacturer partners with role-based access control, configurable workflows, and secure collaboration across labs and organizations.

How it works

A clear path through every case

1

Receive cases, scans, and impressions

Capture digital or physical intake with clinic, practitioner, patient reference, files, scans, products, and due dates in one place. Get real-time turnaround estimates based on order configuration.

2

Coordinate production and communication

Assign work, track statuses, manage alerts, and keep lab, clinic, and manufacturer conversations tied to the case. Use QR code labels to bridge physical work to the digital record.

3

Manage components and shipping

Track shippable components and batches through fulfillment. Prepare documentation, labels, QR codes, and logistics records before the case leaves the lab.

4

Complete invoicing with traceability

Turn products and line items into invoices with real-time pricing. Access full case history, production records, and component tracking for complete traceability.
